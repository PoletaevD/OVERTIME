# Stage 05C sound sources

The WAV files in Assets/Resources/Meridian Audio and the four ARC discharge and reload pairs plus Deadline Charge in Assets/Art/Weapons were rendered specifically for OVERTIME during Stage 05C. The source signals are seeded filtered noise, damped inharmonic resonances, frequency-modulated oscillators and delayed mechanical transients. Each render is mono 32 kHz PCM. The mix includes short reflections and peak limiting.

All 98 clips are original renders, with no sampled third-party material or external attribution requirements. The four firing sets include three variations per weapon. Magazine handling, empty actions, sight movement, hostile cues and four temporal sets use dedicated clips.

Meridian SFX routes weapon handling, spatial threats, hostile movement, impacts, temporal cues, environment and UI through separate mixer groups. Six impact voices, five threat voices and three movement voices are reused. Critical attack warnings use the higher-priority spatial threat pool.
